About DermStratix AI

Clinical decision support architecture for non-invasive cutaneous health screening.

Platform Overview

DermStratix AI bridges modern computer vision and deep transfer learning to assist healthcare professionals in triaging skin lesions and facial cutaneous markers before specialist referral.

Privacy & Data Security

Engineered with privacy-by-design. Image inspection and feature extraction run locally without retaining patient identifiable health information (PHI) on public servers.

Clinical Decision Workflow

Provides pre-inference optical blur verification, automated ABCD criteria quantification, and Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations (LIME) for transparent recommendations.

Intended Clinical Use & Workflow

1
Image Acquisition & Gatekeeping

Optical Laplacian sharpness verification ensures diagnostic image quality before neural processing.

2
Feature Extraction & Transfer Inference

Dual-mode ROI isolation extracts morphological and deep spatial embeddings for multi-class classification.

3
LIME Explainability & Triage

Calibrated posterior probabilities are mapped into three actionable clinical risk tiers with superpixel attribution maps.

Clinical Advisory

DermStratix AI is an investigational clinical decision support platform engineered for preliminary risk stratification and triaging. Automated findings do not constitute a definitive histopathological diagnosis, medical prognosis, or prescription. All screening assessments must be correlated with patient clinical history and verified via in-person examination and biopsy by a licensed healthcare professional.
